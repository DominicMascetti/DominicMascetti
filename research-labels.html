research labels

Label each item as if deciding whether it belongs on the open research page. Jev will be scored against these labels, so go with your gut. Labels are saved in this browser; press export when you're done (or whenever).

publish: substantive AI safety research or analysis you'd want a reader to find: concrete claims, methods, results, or a careful argument.

skip: off-topic, thin, news or announcements, opinion without substance, or slop.

unsure: genuinely borderline. Use sparingly.

keys: p publish · s skip · u unsure · ← → move · n next unlabelled · open the link if the excerpt isn't enough
